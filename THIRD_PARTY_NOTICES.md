## Original work

These assets were made for this project. They carry no embedded copyright notice, and no third-party license was found in the files or in the source:

- `web/sounds/*.wav` are short PCM tones, one file per state, plus the tickle chirp. The page plays those files.
- `web/icons/` are original geometric icons. They are not the Grok logo and not the Home Assistant logo.
- The eye movement in `web/augen.js`, `web/zustaende.js`, and `web/engine.js` is original code. No animation library and no third-party animation repository was copied into this tree.

Nothing was removed or replaced in order to publish under MIT.
